---
title: "BRCC Young Adult Ministry"
---

Research and strategic planning for young adult ministry (ages 22-32) at Brookville Road Community Church in New Palestine, Indiana. This project examines local demographics, national trends, proven ministry models, and BRCC's own context to build a research-based strategy for reaching young adults in Hancock County.
