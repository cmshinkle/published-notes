# Young Adult Ministry Research Project
## Project Overview & Goals

**Project Start Date**: February 2026
**Church**: Brookville Road Community Church (BRCC)
**Location**: 7480 US-52, New Palestine, IN 46163

---

## Primary Goal

Conduct comprehensive research on young adult demographics, trends, and ministry approaches to inform strategic planning for young adult ministry at Brookville Road Community Church.

---

## Target Demographics

**Age Range**: 22-32 years old (post-college through early family formation)

**Ministry Approach**: Integrated young adult ministry serving both singles and young married couples

**Life Stages Included**:
- Young professionals (early career)
- Recent college graduates
- Single young adults
- Young married couples (with and without children)
- Young families (early parenting stage)

**Note**: This range emerged from research synthesis. The focus is post-college young adults navigating career, relationships, and identity formation, distinct from both college-age ministry and established family ministry.

---

## Research Objectives

### 1. **Local Community Research**
- Understand the demographic composition of New Palestine, IN and surrounding communities
- Identify population sizes, growth trends, and characteristics of young adults in the area
- Analyze geographic layers (immediate area, regional, county-level)
- Determine where young adults live, work, and what draws them to the area

### 2. **Surrounding Communities Research**
- Study adjacent towns and cities in southern Hancock County
- Understand the Marion County (Indianapolis) connection and migration patterns
- Identify competition and collaboration opportunities with nearby churches
- Map the broader regional context for ministry

### 3. **National Young Adult Research**
- Research national data on 22-32 year old demographic
- Understand generational characteristics (Millennials, Gen Z)
- Identify life stage challenges, spiritual needs, and cultural trends
- Examine socioeconomic factors, education, employment, and family formation patterns

### 4. **National Young Adult Ministry Research**
- Study successful young adult ministry models from churches across the country
- Identify best practices, common challenges, and innovative approaches
- Understand what draws young adults to church and what keeps them engaged
- Research programming ideas, community-building strategies, and discipleship models

### 5. **Strategic Analysis and Options**
- Analyze ministry model options in BRCC's specific context
- Examine approaches used by churches of similar size, location, and resources
- Identify strategic considerations for outreach, programming, and community building
- Document common challenges and success factors from research

## Notes and Context

### Geographic Context
- BRCC is located in southern Hancock County, IN
- Less than a few miles west is Marion County (Indianapolis metro area)
- Most of BRCC's congregation lives in this southern Hancock/western Marion County area
- The church is positioned at the intersection of rural small town (New Palestine) and suburban Indianapolis growth

### Church Context
- BRCC is a non-denominational church founded in 1981
- Current congregation: 1,000+ people
- Strong family orientation with existing children's and youth programs
- Mission: "Inspire people to become wholehearted followers of Jesus"
- Vision: "Impact and renew New Palestine and beyond with the transforming message of Jesus Christ"

### Cultural Context
- Post-COVID church landscape with changing attendance patterns
- Digital-native generations (Millennials, Gen Z) with different engagement preferences
- Economic pressures on young adults (housing costs, student loans, etc.)
- Growing suburban population in Hancock County area
