# Brookville Road Community Church (BRCC)
## Church Profile

**Official Name**: Brookville Road Community Church
**Common Name**: BRCC
**Address**: 7480 US-52 (West US-52), New Palestine, IN 46163
**Phone**: (317) 861-3880
**Website**: [www.brookvilleroad.cc](https://www.brookvilleroad.cc/)

---

## Overview

Brookville Road Community Church is a non-denominational Christian church located in New Palestine, Indiana, serving the southern Hancock County and western Marion County communities.

---

## History

**Founded**: 1981
**Age**: 40+ years of ministry in the community
**Growth**: Has grown into a church family of over 1,000 people

---

## Mission, Vision, and Values

### Mission Statement
**"Inspire people to become wholehearted followers of Jesus"**

### Vision Statement
**"Impact and renew New Palestine and beyond with the transforming message of Jesus Christ through words and actions"**

### Core Values

1. **Christ-Centered**: Focus on Jesus as the foundation of faith and ministry
2. **Biblically-Based**: Grounded in Scripture and biblical teaching
3. **Discipleship**: Committed to spiritual growth and following Jesus
4. **Engagement**: Actively inspiring people to become followers
5. **Authenticity**: Wholehearted, genuine faith and community
6. **Community**: Fostering love for the church and one another
7. **Mission-Minded**: Focused on loving and serving the world

## Potential Challenges for Young Adult Ministry

### 1. Small Town Context
- New Palestine proper is only 3,700 people
- May feel too "small town" for urban-oriented young adults
- Limited entertainment, dining, social options compared to Indianapolis

### 2. Current Age Distribution
- 1,000+ members, but current young adult percentage unknown
- May be heavily weighted toward families with children and older adults
- Potential cultural gap between generations

### 3. Competition
- Other churches in area likely targeting same demographic
- Indianapolis churches may draw young professionals
- Megachurches with extensive programming

### 4. Awareness
- Young adults moving to area may not know about BRCC
- Digital presence and discoverability unknown
- Marketing to new residents is ongoing challenge

### 5. Programming
- Unknown if current programs appeal to young adults
- May need to develop new ministry models
- Balancing traditional church culture with young adult preferences
